## 5. Editorial Voice & Copywriting Rules

1. **Direct, Technical, Unhurried:** Write like a peer-reviewed engineering studio (Anthropic, Linear, Modal, DeepMind). Let concrete architecture carry the authority.
2. **Zero Hype Inflation:** Ban empty superlatives ("revolutionary", "groundbreaking", "world-changing", "disruptive", "unmatched", "AGI-level") and unnecessary exclamation marks.
3. **Evidence-Attached Claims:** Every capability claim must state whether it is **Verified / Public**, **In Active Development**, **Experimental**, **Planned**, or **Research Vision**.
4. **No Fake Social Proof:** Never publish invented customer logos, testimonials, download counts, enterprise SLAs, or SOC 2 / ISO / HIPAA compliance badges.
5. **Respect Third-Party Provenance:** Always attribute open-weight foundations (`Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B`, `openai/gpt-oss-120b`) transparently and note that third-party product/engine names indicate experimentation or target compatibility, never endorsement or legal partnership.
