## 4. Unit Economics, Infrastructure & Commercial Viability Factors

Launching sustainable commercial AI products requires aligning pricing, rate limits, and deployment architecture to real 2026 GPU inference and support costs:
- **Inference & GPU Economics (Nov 2026 Market Benchmarks):** Serverless per-second GPU providers (e.g., Modal) price H100 compute around ~\$3.95/GPU-hr, A100 80GB around ~\$2.50/GPU-hr, and L4 around ~\$0.80/GPU-hr, scaling to zero during idle periods; reserved clusters become cheaper only above ~80% sustained utilization.
- **27B vs. 120B Tier Separation:** Running `Hackmanite Prime` (27B dense, FP8/quantized on single-GPU or local developer workstations) carries dramatically lower inference cost per million tokens than `Hackmanite Reasoning` (120B class), making task-aware routing (`Auto` mode) essential to protect gross margins.
- **Flat-Fee Trap Mitigation:** Industry-wide agentic coding workloads in 2026 demonstrated that uncapped flat-fee subscriptions suffer 10×–50× cost spikes under heavy autonomous loops. FDS commercial architectures must pair transparent usage envelopes, local-model offloading (Jaspilite), and user-supplied subscription routing rather than unsustainable "unlimited" promises.
- **Licensing Advantage:** Both proposed Hackmanite base foundations carry unmodified **Apache 2.0** licenses, avoiding restrictive revenue caps or non-commercial clauses when FDS ships certified commercial adaptations.
- **Zero Premature Sales Commitments:** No checkout flow, subscription billing portal, or binding SLA is enabled on `forgerdigitalsolutions.com` until underlying models and installers clear independent certification and owner commercial approval.
