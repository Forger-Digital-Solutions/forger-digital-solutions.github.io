Welcome to **Notes** — the writing side of Forger Digital Solutions.

Most of what happens at FDS lives in code, experiments, and long feedback loops. Notes is where some of that work comes up for air: short write-ups on what we're building, what we're learning, and the reasoning behind decisions.

## What to expect here

- **Build logs** — progress on projects like GEMS, KyraBlox, and the rest.
- **Research notes** — ideas we're testing, and what the evidence actually says.
- **Process** — how we think about tooling, evaluation, and shipping.

Nothing here is a product announcement or a promise of a shipping date. It's a record of work in progress — written in the same spirit as the [Lab](/lab): build, test, measure, learn, evolve.
